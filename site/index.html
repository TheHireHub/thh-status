About this page

Is TheHireHub down right now?

The banner at the top of this page is the answer. Green means every service passed its last check. Any outage or slowdown shows here within five minutes, with an incident entry below.

How often is TheHireHub's status checked?

Every 5 minutes, from outside our own infrastructure. This page is hosted independently of thehirehub.ai so it stays up even when our main systems don't.

What do the services mean?

Website is thehirehub.ai. Platform is the hiring workspace you log in to. API powers the platform, integrations and AiRA. Media serves resumes, uploads and interview recordings.

How do I report a problem?

If something looks wrong and no incident is listed, contact us and we'll investigate.
